About

Colin Dowling

I’ve spent twenty years in B2B revenue. VP Enterprise Sales at GoDaddy. SVP Revenue at Alembic, from seed through a $145M Series B. VP Channel Sales at Liquid Web. Enterprise Sales Director at WP Engine. Three times a VP of Sales, twice a founder.

I’ve built go-to-market motions from nothing, and I’ve inherited ones that looked fine on a dashboard and were quietly broken underneath. I’ve hired sellers who worked out and sellers who didn’t. I’ve been the person carrying the number, which is the part that matters most when I’m talking to someone who’s carrying theirs.

Why I only advise

For a while I ran a version of this that did everything for the client — built the system, ran the outbound, managed the pipeline. It worked. I didn’t like it.

What I noticed is that the engagements I was proud of all looked the same: the owner was in it, doing the work, using me to pressure-test decisions and to shortcut the mistakes. The ones that went badly were the ones where someone wanted to hand me the problem and get a number back. Not because those people were difficult — because it’s the wrong shape. Nobody outsources the thing their business runs on and comes out the other side owning it.

So now I do one thing. An hour a week, six people at a time, teaching owners how to build the motion and making sure they actually build it.

What I’m like to work with

Direct. I’ll tell you when something you’re proud of isn’t working, and I’ll tell you when the thing you’re worried about doesn’t matter. I don’t pad an hour to feel valuable, and if we get through the agenda in forty minutes we’ll finish in forty minutes.

I’ll also ask what you committed to last week, every week, which some people find motivating and some people find irritating. It’s the same question either way.

Other things

I host Modern Business Life, a podcast about how people actually run companies.

I’m based in Dallas–Fort Worth.
