The Problem

Five symptoms. One underlying problem.

01

Outbound that burns out.

You send, it works for a month, then it stops. No trigger telling you who’s worth contacting this week, and a target list wide enough that nothing you write lands as specific.

02

Content nobody buys from.

You’re publishing. It isn’t mapped to how your buyers actually decide, so it gets read by people who will never purchase and missed by the ones comparing you to two competitors right now.

03

Channels that run in parallel instead of together.

Email, content, LinkedIn, referrals — four separate efforts with four separate lists, none of them compounding into the others.

04

Deals that slip.

Follow-up lives in your head and in your inbox. The ones that go quiet stay quiet, and you find out in the forecast three weeks later.

05

A number you don’t trust.

Stages mean different things on different days, the pipeline includes deals that died in April, and you’re guessing at the quarter.

These aren’t five problems. They’re five symptoms of a motion that was assembled a piece at a time and never designed. Which is also why fixing them in the wrong order didn’t work.

The Method

Score it. Work it. Re-score it.

01

Score it.

Your go-to-market against 100 dimensions, each marked pass, partial or fail, ranked by what it’s costing you. Delivered with a recorded walkthrough. Nobody scores 100.

02

Work it.

An hour a week. We review what happened, recalibrate where the plan was wrong rather than the execution, set the week, and spend the last twenty minutes on whichever part of the motion your score says is weakest. Anything we build, we build together on the call.

03

Re-score it.

Every quarter. The movement is the report. A motion that scored well in March can score worse in September. A competitor repositions, you make your first marketing hire, a channel stops working. That’s the point. This doesn’t finish. Success is a journey.
