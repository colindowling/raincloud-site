01. Positioning and messaging

14 points. 10 externally scoreable. Marked Outside-in.

  1. 001 One sentence states who the company is for and why it wins. Outside-in
  2. 002 Positioning names the real alternatives, including doing nothing. Outside-in
  3. 003 Differentiation is specific and provable, not adjectives. Outside-in
  4. 004 Messaging is written by persona, not one message for every buyer. Outside-in
  5. 005 Every claim is mapped to a customer, a number, or a case. Outside-in
  6. 006 That proof sits next to the claim, not only in a sales folder. Outside-in
  7. 007 Website, decks, sequences and reps say the same thing. Outside-in
  8. 008 A buyer can tell the company apart from its top two competitors before the first call. Outside-in
  9. 009 Customers describe the value in the same terms the company uses. Outside-in
  10. 010 Each offer has a written scope and a written outcome. Outside-in
  11. 011 The top five objections have written, tested responses.
  12. 012 Messaging has been tested against reply, meeting or conversion data in the last two quarters.
  13. 013 A defined entry offer lowers the risk of the first purchase.
  14. 014 The offer gives a buyer a reason to act this quarter.

02. ICP and TAM definition

12 points. 6 externally scoreable. Marked Outside-in.

  1. 015 A written ICP is specific enough that a rep can disqualify an account with it. Outside-in
  2. 016 Firmographic boundaries name a revenue band and a headcount range. Outside-in
  3. 017 Firmographic boundaries name industry and geography. Outside-in
  4. 018 The buying triggers that put an account in-market now are named. Outside-in
  5. 019 The buying committee is mapped: economic buyer, champion, likely blockers. Outside-in
  6. 020 A target account list exists. Outside-in
  7. 021 The ICP is derived from closed-won data, not from aspiration.
  8. 022 Written disqualification criteria exist and are used.
  9. 023 The target account list is sized and prioritized against the ICP.
  10. 024 Win rate, deal size and cycle length are known by ICP segment.
  11. 025 The ICP is re-checked against new closed-won and closed-lost data at least twice a year.
  12. 026 Sales, marketing and leadership describe the same ICP when asked separately.

03. Demand generation, top of funnel

14 points. 8 externally scoreable. Marked Outside-in.

  1. 027 The website says who it is for, above the fold. Outside-in
  2. 028 The website says what to do next, above the fold. Outside-in
  3. 029 Content exists for buyers who are not yet in a deal. Outside-in
  4. 030 That content is mapped to the early stages of the buying journey. Outside-in
  5. 031 The problem the company creates demand around is named in public. Outside-in
  6. 032 Content aimed at that problem is published on a cadence, not only when a live deal needs a page. Outside-in
  7. 033 Top-of-funnel demand is aimed at the written ICP. Outside-in
  8. 034 More than one channel creates demand before a buyer raises a hand. Outside-in
  9. 035 A written content plan covers buyers who are not in a live deal.
  10. 036 Top-of-funnel activity is attributed separately from outbound and from inbound response.
  11. 037 One person owns top-of-funnel demand as a number.
  12. 038 Sales and marketing share targets and meet on them.
  13. 039 References and case studies are captured systematically for later use.
  14. 040 Early-stage content does not assume the reader is already in a sales cycle.

04. Consideration and bottom-of-funnel presence

12 points. 10 externally scoreable. Marked Outside-in.

  1. 041 A pricing page exists. Outside-in
  2. 042 Comparison pages exist against the real alternatives. Outside-in
  3. 043 Use-case pages exist for the way the ICP buys. Outside-in
  4. 044 The bottom-of-funnel search terms buyers actually use are targeted. Outside-in
  5. 045 Results and numbers are easy to find on the site. Outside-in
  6. 046 Case studies are easy to find on the site. Outside-in
  7. 047 A buyer searching the problem in AI answers finds the company, or a clear gap. Outside-in
  8. 048 The company is present in the directories a buyer in this category would check. Outside-in
  9. 049 Reviews a buyer would read are findable. Outside-in
  10. 050 The company shows up, or is clearly absent, in the forums where buyers ask. Outside-in
  11. 051 Pricing has been reviewed against competitors and win-loss data in the last year.
  12. 052 Battlecards exist for the top competitors and are current.

05. Outbound motion

12 points. 4 externally scoreable. Marked Outside-in.

  1. 053 Outbound targets the stated ICP, not a random bought list. Outside-in
  2. 054 Copy is written by persona and references something specific to the account. Outside-in
  3. 055 Sending uses authenticated domains rather than a freemail identity. Outside-in
  4. 056 The channels in the motion match what the company says in public. Outside-in
  5. 057 Signals or triggers decide who gets contacted, and those signals are named.
  6. 058 Signals acted on are under seven days old.
  7. 059 Sequence architecture is documented: steps, timing, and the writing.
  8. 060 Warm-up is configured and monitored.
  9. 061 Bounce, reply and spam-complaint rates are tracked against thresholds.
  10. 062 Meetings booked and meetings held are tracked separately.
  11. 063 Outbound-sourced pipeline and revenue are attributable in the CRM.
  12. 064 Sequences are tested and retired on data at least quarterly.

06. Inbound handling and speed to lead

10 points. 2 externally scoreable. Marked Outside-in.

  1. 065 A form or other request path is present on high-intent pages. Outside-in
  2. 066 The site states what happens after a request, including who it reaches. Outside-in
  3. 067 Inbound leads reach a person inside a defined time.
  4. 068 Inbound response time is measured.
  5. 069 That measured time is compared with the standard the company set.
  6. 070 Form conversion rates are measured by page.
  7. 071 Lead source is captured consistently on every record.
  8. 072 Inbound-sourced pipeline is reported monthly.
  9. 073 Inbound-sourced revenue is reported monthly.
  10. 074 Replies are routed and worked inside a defined time.

07. Sales process and pipeline hygiene

14 points. 0 externally scoreable. Marked Outside-in.

  1. 075 Stage definitions have written entry and exit criteria and mean the same thing to every rep.
  2. 076 Conversion rates between stages are measured by segment, and the volume the number requires is calculated from those rates.
  3. 077 Sales cycle length is measured by segment and deal size.
  4. 078 Coverage is calculated on qualified opportunities only, and the forecast describes a number the pipeline can produce.
  5. 079 Forecast categories are defined, applied consistently, and accuracy is measured against actuals.
  6. 080 Deals with no recent activity are flagged or closed out, and every open opportunity has a dated next step.
  7. 081 A written discovery framework is used on first calls, and qualification criteria are recorded in the CRM.
  8. 082 A mutual action plan is used on deals above a set size, and deal reviews follow a consistent format.
  9. 083 Win-loss reasons are captured on every closed deal, and where deals stall is known by stage.
  10. 084 Win rate is measured by segment, source and rep, and rep ramp, onboarding and call coaching are defined.
  11. 085 The handoff from sales to delivery or onboarding is documented.
  12. 086 Discounting runs on written rules, average discount is tracked, and price objections are tracked by stage and reason.
  13. 087 Pricing logic is documented, packaging matches how the ICP buys, and reps can explain pricing without escalating.
  14. 088 One executive owns the revenue number, quotas come from capacity math, and the weekly operating cadence logs its decisions.

08. Data, CRM and revenue operations

12 points. 0 externally scoreable. Marked Outside-in.

  1. 089 The CRM is the single source of truth for pipeline, and sales and marketing share one definition of lead, qualified lead and opportunity.
  2. 090 Required fields are enforced at each stage, and completeness of key fields is measured.
  3. 091 Duplicate records are cleaned on a schedule.
  4. 092 Stale records are cleaned on a schedule.
  5. 093 Pipeline and conversion dashboards exist and are used, and core reports run without manual spreadsheet work.
  6. 094 Every tool in the go-to-market stack has an owner and measured use, and tool spend is reviewed against usage at least yearly.
  7. 095 Attribution rules are written and applied consistently.
  8. 096 Gross and net revenue retention are measured, and one person owns retention and expansion as a number.
  9. 097 Onboarding has a defined time to first value, and customer health is scored on defined signals.
  10. 098 Churn reasons are captured, and renewal dates are in the CRM with enough lead time to act.
  11. 099 Expansion paths are defined by customer type, and expansion pipeline is tracked separately from new business.
  12. 100 Customer feedback reaches positioning and product decisions.
