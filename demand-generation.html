FAQ

Straight answers.

What counts as an Engine-sourced deal?

A deal where the first qualified conversation came from Hunter, Harvester, or Farmer, or that was meaningfully advanced by them. The attribution rules, and how disputes are handled, are agreed in writing before we start. Contact source is also recorded on every lead, so there is a clean record.

What if I do not have a foundation yet?

Then the GTM Audit is the entry. Nothing in the Engine works well without a tight ICP and an offer matrix, so I do not skip it.

Why a revenue share instead of a flat retainer?

Because I would rather earn more only when you close more. The launch fee covers setup, message testing, and baselines. After that, the fee scales with the deals the Engine actually sources.

What is Shepherd and do I need it?

Shepherd is short-form social video that guides attention to your site. It is an optional add-on, fulfilled with a partner. Most programs start without it.

Is this compliant with email and LinkedIn rules?

I review each program against email and LinkedIn rules before anything goes out, and I own that review. If something does not pass, it does not ship.

How is this different from Content?

Content is Farmer on its own, plus ghostwriting. Demand Generation is the whole Engine: Hunter, Harvester, and Farmer together, with Farmer content included. If you want outbound, LinkedIn, and content running as one system, this is the one.
