What the data actually says.

The instinct is to blame the copy. It is almost never the copy.

Say what you divided by

If you are going to quote a reply rate to your board, say what you divided by. Most people quoting these numbers cannot tell you.

Check the DNS first

If your reply rate fell off a cliff somewhere in 2024 or 2025 and never recovered, check your DNS records before you rewrite a single sentence of copy.

344 cold emails per meeting booked

Analysis of 85 million cold emails by Gong and 30 Minutes to President’s Club found the average rep now sends 344 cold emails per meeting booked.

Not a dead channel

A market where the best operators outperform the average by 8x is not a dead channel. It is a channel that stopped rewarding volume and started rewarding craft.

Outbound.

Targeting, sequence architecture, deliverability, and what actually happens after a reply.

  • Outbound targets the ICP and the target account list, not bought lists at random
  • Signals or triggers decide who gets contacted and when
  • Sequence architecture is documented: steps, channels, timing
  • Copy is written by persona and references something specific to the account
  • Sending domains, authentication and warm-up are configured and monitored
  • Bounce, reply and spam-complaint rates are tracked against thresholds
  • Replies are routed and worked inside a defined time
  • Meetings booked and meetings held are tracked separately
  • Outbound-sourced pipeline and revenue are attributable in the CRM
  • Sequences are tested and retired on data at least quarterly

Is outbound dead?

Outbound is not dead. It has become an infrastructure discipline wearing a copywriting costume. The teams still getting 10% replies are not writing better subject lines than you. They authenticated properly, they send less, they send from clean domains to smaller lists, and they pick up the phone.
