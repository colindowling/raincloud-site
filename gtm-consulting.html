FAQ

The obvious questions.

Why is there a six-month minimum?

Three months of build, three months of advisory. You are committing to the handover, not just the build. A motion that gets rebuilt and then left alone goes back to where it was.

What is the difference between the two ongoing tiers?

Advisory ($7,500/mo) includes a monthly work session and weekly office hours, so you get working time. The GTM Scorecard ($4,000/mo, billed quarterly) is measurement and direction only: the monthly review, the mid-month check, the quarterly re-score.

Do I need to do the GTM Audit first?

Yes. Nothing is sold before the audit. It is $5,000, ten business days, and not credited toward the build. I will not sell a six-month engagement off a sales call, and you should not buy one.

Who actually does the work?

I do. There is no delivery team, no junior analyst, and no offshore bench. That is also why I run two builds at a time.
