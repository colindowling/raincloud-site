How the 90 days work

Three months, each with one job.

Working sessions through the build, not status calls. Everything is built where your data lives, so nothing depends on a spreadsheet I maintain.

Month 1

Diagnose + rebuild

The audit gave us the score and the order. Month one turns the first findings into the foundation everything else stands on: who you sell to, why they buy from you, and the baseline you’ll be measured against.

What ships
  • ICP rebuilt from closed-won data
  • Target account list
  • Positioning and message map by persona
  • Objection handling
  • Baseline locked and signed
Month 2

Install

The foundation goes into the machinery. Stage definitions your reps will actually use, the volume your number really requires, and an outbound motion aimed at the ICP from month one — signal-based email, LinkedIn engagement and bottom-of-funnel content working together.

What ships
  • Pipeline model with stage exit criteria and derived volume targets
  • Outbound build — list sources, sequence architecture, written sequences, deliverability configured
  • Scorecard built in your CRM
Month 3

Prove + hand off

The motion runs while I’m still in it. We tune it against the signed baseline, equip the team to run deals the same way, and write it down well enough that it survives without me.

What ships
  • Sales operating kit — discovery framework, qualification criteria, battlecards, deal review format
  • Optimization against the baseline
  • The 90-day playbook and operating cadence documented

Everything above lives in your tools and stays yours whether or not we keep working together. See the month-by-month fees

After day 90

The work changes hands. So does the fee.

From month four your team runs the motion and my job becomes measuring it: finding what’s leaking, saying what to do about it, naming who owns it and by when, and telling you the truth about whether it’s working.

Most GTM builds fail in the quarter after the consultant leaves. The handover isn’t the end of the engagement — it’s the half that’s priced to last.

FAQ

Questions about the work.

Questions about price, terms, refunds and the minimum are answered on Pricing.

How is this page different from Pricing?

Pricing covers what it costs and the terms. This page covers the work: what happens in each of the 90 days, what changes hands at the end, and what it asks of your team.

What does my team need to give you during the build?

One named executive sponsor for the full 90 days, read-only CRM access from day one, and the people who will run the motion afterwards in the room when it is built. If the sponsor changes, we re-charter.

Do we need to buy new tools?

The build is done in your CRM and your existing stack wherever possible. Paid media spend, tooling and data subscriptions stay yours, so anything new is a decision you make with the cost in front of you.

Will you run our outbound or manage our reps?

No. I build the motion and your team runs it. I don’t manage your reps, carry a quota or sit in your pipeline calls as an operator. That is why the fee falls when the work changes hands.

Who actually does the work?

I do. There is no delivery team, no junior analyst, and no offshore bench. That is also why I run two builds at a time.

What happens at the end of month three?

Your team takes over the running of it and my job becomes measuring it: the monthly operating review, a work session, office hours and the quarterly re-score. The fee steps down to the ongoing rate. The tiers and terms are on the pricing page.
