FAQ

The obvious questions.

Why is the Hold phase mandatory?

Most GTM rebuilds fail in the quarter after the consultant leaves. The system drifts, nobody owns the calls that keep it tuned, and the team reverts to old habits. The Hold phase exists to stop that, so it is priced and contracted with the Fix as one engagement.

What is the difference between Foundations and the Series A tier?

Foundations is advisory-led: I am on the call, not embedded in the build. It suits pre-seed and seed teams that need real GTM guidance before they can carry a full rebuild. Series A is the full Fix and Hold, with me in the work two sessions a week for 90 days.

Do I need to do the GTM Audit first?

It is the recommended entry point, and the $7,500 credits in full toward the engagement. If you already know what is broken, we can talk about skipping it.

Who actually does the work?

I do. There is no delivery team, no junior analyst, and no offshore bench. Where a part of the work is better run by software, I hand it to the Engine or to AI agents, and I run those too.
