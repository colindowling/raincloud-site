The Outside-In GTM Teardown. A free sales pipeline audit from the outside.

The 20 Minute Teardown is a free 40-point outside-in review. It scores the points of the 100-Point GTM Autopsy that can be read from public sources alone. No CRM access. No interviews. Aimed at a CRO, VP of Sales, VP of Marketing, or a founder still close to revenue.
