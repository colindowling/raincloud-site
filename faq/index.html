Common questions.

Why is the audit required?

Because the 100-Point GTM Autopsy is the build specification, and a build without one is a project priced on a guess. Nothing skips it.

How does the audit credit work?

The $5,000 is credited in full against the $25,000 Build, so the Build costs $20,000 net.

Can we skip the audit if we already know what's wrong?

No. You may be right about one or two things and miss the rest. Every Build starts with the 100-Point GTM Autopsy.

Is our data secure?

RAINCLOUD writes activity, contacts and opportunity stage changes only, using field mappings agreed during the Build. No schema changes and no custom objects.

How is this different from a free assessment?

A free assessment is a sales call with a slide at the end. The free Outside-In GTM Teardown scores 40 points from public sources. The 100-Point GTM Autopsy scores all 100 points inside your systems.

Who actually does the work?

Colin Dowling personally runs every 100-Point GTM Autopsy and every monthly operating review. RAINCLOUD runs the system on your CRM. Capacity is four clients at a time.

What if our outbound isn't running yet?

That is fine. An outbound motion that does not exist is a finding. The 100-Point GTM Autopsy still scores the rest of the motion.

Do you work with companies outside $50M to $200M?

The fit is B2B revenue of $50M to $200M and an average contract value around $50,000 or higher. Outside that, ask on the 20 Minute Teardown.

Do you run our outbound, and do you manage our reps?

RAINCLOUD builds and runs the pipeline on your CRM. It does not close deals and it does not manage your reps.

Are domains, mailboxes and tools included?

Yes. Infrastructure is included in the $10,000 a month. Sending domains are registered in your company's name. There are no pass-through line items for domains or mailboxes.

What happens if the agreed day-90 pipeline target is missed?

If the agreed day-90 pipeline target is missed, RAINCLOUD keeps running the system at no charge until it is hit. No cash refund. The paid Autopsy is the trial.

What is the free Outside-In GTM Teardown?

The 20 Minute Teardown is the free Outside-In GTM Teardown. It scores the 40 points of the 100-Point GTM Autopsy that can be read from public sources. Book it at https://calendly.com/30minutechat/20-minute-go-to-market-w-colin.

What does a sales process audit cover?

The sales process audit is the 100-Point GTM Autopsy. Every point is pass, partial or fail, ranked by revenue impact, with the ten highest-impact gaps first. It is delivered five business days after access is granted, with one recorded 90-minute walkthrough.

How much does B2B lead generation cost with RAINCLOUD?

The 100-Point GTM Autopsy is $5,000. The Build is $25,000, or $20,000 after the audit credit. Ongoing operation is $10,000 a month. The first seven months total $85,000. I do not quote a market rate.

When does the six-month minimum start?

The six-month minimum starts at go-live, not at signature.

How is billing handled?

Ongoing operation is billed quarterly in advance, as two payments of $30,000.

Who owns the sending domains?

Sending domains are registered in your company's name. You approve the full list in writing before anything is sent.

What do you need from us within ten business days of signature?

CRM access, domains registered in the company's name, approval of the sending-domain list, and a named point of contact, all within ten business days of signature. If that slips, the build clock and the agreed day-90 pipeline target move.
