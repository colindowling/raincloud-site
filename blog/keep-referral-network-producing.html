Referrals

How to get more B2B referrals

Colin Dowling November 2026 6 min read
Key takeaways
  1. Referral networks decay when contact is accidental.
  2. Work a short list of people who actually send deals.
  3. Make the referral easy to describe, and close the loop every time.
  4. Give introductions before you ask for them.

How to get more B2B referrals is a pipeline question, not a luck question. A referral network keeps producing when a short list of the right people hears from you on purpose, knows exactly who to send, and hears what happened after they send them. Left alone, it fades.

Networks start strong because the relationships are fresh. Then everyone gets busy. A year later the list is names that vaguely remember you.

Why do referral networks fade?

People refer whoever is easy to describe and recently in mind. If nobody owns the work, it feels like luck, so nobody schedules it. Contacts change jobs. Partners stop being sure who you help. They never hear how the last introduction went, so they stop making the next one.

All of that is operational. It is not a personality trait.

What does a simple system look like?

Pick the few people who have actually sent business, or who sit next to your best customers. A long list is a newsletter. A short list is a channel.

Tell them, in one or two sentences, who you help and what situation should make them think of you. If they have to invent the description, they will not.

Close the loop. Thank them. Tell them what happened, including when it did not become a deal. Then look for a way to return the introduction. Partners who hear back keep sending.

Put an owner on it. Referrals that sit outside the motion get forgotten the week the quarter gets loud. Inside the motion, they are a source with a rhythm, the same way outbound is. That is part of what the 90-day engagement installs, after you know the motion is worth installing.

Questions this post answers

How do I get more B2B referrals?

Work a short list of people who already know your best customers. Tell them who you help and what situation should trigger a referral. Thank them when they send someone, and make introductions for them first.

How often should I contact referral partners?

A useful touch on a steady rhythm, and a real conversation on a longer one. Useful means an introduction, an idea, or news about a mutual contact. A generic newsletter does not count.

Why did referrals dry up?

The relationships were left alone. People forget who does what, and they refer whoever they spoke to last. A small system usually brings a faded network back. A fee is a separate choice, and it is not required for this to work.
