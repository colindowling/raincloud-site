What fractional CRO cost means here

Colin Dowling November 2026 5 min read
Key takeaways
  1. There is no market rate on this page, because I will not invent one.
  2. The teardown is free. The Scorecard is $5,000. The Standing Hour is $4,500 a month.
  3. Maintenance is $2,500 a month, every other week, after the Milestone Analysis.
  4. There is no guarantee. The work is yours and so is the result.

Fractional CRO cost, on this site, means the fees I publish. I will not invent a salary, a day rate, or a market average for the category. Anyone who gives you a single number for "a fractional CRO" is averaging jobs that are not the same job.

What I charge is short. The 20 Minute Teardown is free. The 100-Point Scorecard is $5,000 and takes five business days. The Standing Hour is $4,500 a month.

What do those numbers buy?

The teardown covers roughly 40 of the 100 points I can read from public sources. It is not the paid diagnostic.

The Scorecard scores all 100 points, including the sixty I can't see from outside. You get a written scorecard, findings ranked by revenue impact, and a recorded ninety-minute walkthrough. It is $5,000 standalone, or it takes month one at no extra cost. The Scorecard page is the scope. Pricing is the schedule.

The Standing Hour is $4,500 a month. Eleven sessions a quarter, same slot every week, plus office hours twice a week. Three-month minimum if you start with the Scorecard. Six if you don't. Maintenance, after your first term if it fits, is $2,500 a month, every other week.

What is not included in the price?

A full-time VP. A recruited placement. An agency that sends for you. I do not manage your reps. Fractional CRO vs VP of sales is that line. What a fractional CRO does here is the job, not the invoice. If what you need is someone to help you fix what you’re already doing, that’s the Standing Hour.

Twenty minutes, nothing to prepare, and you keep the scorecard either way.

Questions this post answers

How much does a fractional CRO cost?

I publish my fees only. The 20 Minute Teardown is free. The 100-Point Scorecard is $5,000. The Standing Hour is $4,500 a month. I do not quote anyone else's rate.

Is there a cheaper version?

One price. I don't discount it, and there isn't a cheaper version. At the end of your first term, the Milestone Analysis may recommend maintenance at $2,500 every other week.

Is there a guarantee?

No. I don't guarantee a revenue outcome, because the work is yours and so is the result. The Scorecard is $5,000 standalone, or it takes month one at no extra cost.
