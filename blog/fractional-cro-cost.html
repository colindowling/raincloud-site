What fractional CRO cost means here

Colin Dowling November 2026 5 min read
Key takeaways
  1. There is no market rate on this page, because I will not invent one.
  2. The teardown is free. The autopsy is $5,000. The 90 days are $37,500.
  3. Advisory is $7,500 a month from month four. The Scorecard is $4,000 from month seven.
  4. The autopsy fee is credited in full and has a no-questions-asked money-back guarantee.

Fractional CRO cost, on this site, means the fees I publish. I will not invent a salary, a day rate, or a market average for the category. Anyone who gives you a single number for "a fractional CRO" is averaging jobs that are not the same job.

What I charge is short. The 20 Minute Teardown is free. The 100-Point GTM Autopsy is $5,000 and takes ten business days. The 90-day engagement is $37,500.

What do those numbers buy?

The teardown covers roughly 40 of the 100 points I can read from public sources. It is not the paid diagnostic.

The autopsy scores all 100 points against your CRM and your team. You get a baseline, every finding ranked by what it costs in pipeline, and a one-page business case. The $5,000 is credited in full toward any engagement, and it has a no-questions-asked money-back guarantee. The autopsy page is the scope. Pricing is the schedule.

The 90 days are $15,000, then $12,500, then $10,000. Advisory is $7,500 a month from month four. The GTM Scorecard is $4,000 a month, billed quarterly, from month seven or directly after an autopsy. The six-month minimum is three months of build and three of advisory.

What is not included in the price?

A full-time VP. A recruited placement. An agency that keeps sending after I leave. I do not manage your reps. Fractional CRO vs VP of sales is that line. What a fractional CRO does here is the job, not the invoice.

The list is free. The score is the 100-Point GTM Autopsy.

Every point I score, published in full. It’s the full list the 100-Point GTM Autopsy scores against, so you can see exactly what it measures before you spend anything. The 20 Minute Teardown covers the slice that can be scored from outside the company.

Questions this post answers

How much does a fractional CRO cost?

I publish my fees only. The 20 Minute Teardown is free. The 100-Point GTM Autopsy is $5,000. The 90-day engagement is $37,500, billed $15,000, then $12,500, then $10,000. I do not quote anyone else's rate.

Why does the fee go down?

Because the work changes. Month one is the heavy rebuild. From month four your team runs it and advisory is $7,500 a month. The Scorecard, measurement only, is $4,000 a month from month seven, or right after an autopsy.
