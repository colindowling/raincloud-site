Fractional CRO

What does a fractional CRO do?

Colin Dowling November 2026 6 min read
Key takeaways
  1. A fractional CRO is a part-time revenue operator, not a full-time employee.
  2. The useful version rebuilds the motion and leaves it with your team.
  3. An open-ended seat that manages reps is a different purchase.
  4. I start with a free teardown, then a $5,000 autopsy, then a 90-day build.

A fractional CRO scores the revenue motion and rebuilds the parts your team will run, instead of joining as a full-time chief revenue officer. I do that work myself. It starts with a free 20 Minute Teardown, then the 100-Point GTM Autopsy if the teardown is useful.

The title is used loosely. Some buyers want a coach for the VP. Some want a firm to assign them a person. Some want the motion fixed and handed back. Only the last one is what I sell.

What does the work look like?

Month one is the foundation: who you sell to, the message, the baseline. Month two puts it in the CRM and the outbound. Month three writes it down so it survives. The detail is on the engagement page. The fees are on the pricing page.

I run two builds at a time. There is no bench. If you want a roster, that is a network, and I am the alternative to that.

What does a fractional CRO not do?

I do not carry a quota. I do not manage reps day to day. I do not recruit your next VP. After day 90 your team runs the motion. Advisory, if you want it, starts at month four and is priced on the pricing page.

If the company is still one founder and a first seller, you may not need this title at all. Read the fractional sales leader page and when the first sales hire is not working.

Questions this post answers

What does a fractional CRO do day to day?

On an engagement with me, the days are the audit and then the 90-day rebuild. ICP, positioning, the pipeline model, outbound, the scorecard in your CRM, and the playbook. I do not sit in as the manager of your reps.

Is a fractional CRO the same as a VP of sales?

No. A VP is a full-time employee who runs the team. I do not take that job. The comparison is on the fractional CRO vs VP of sales page.
