How to get out of founder-led sales starts with a hard fact. It works, which is why it lasts. You are the qualification, the story, and the close. The team treats the system as optional because the founder is the system.

Leaving that role is not a hiring announcement. It is a document. If a new person cannot explain who the company is for, and why a buyer should switch, another seller adds meetings you still have to save.

## What has to exist before you step back?

Four things, written, not implied.

Who you sell to, tight enough that a rep can say no to an account. The [founder-led sales trap](/problems/founder-led-sales-trap/) is what happens when that line stays in your head.

What a qualified deal is, and the dated next step every open opportunity must have.

The story you tell on the first call, in words someone else can say. [Handing the founder story to the team](/blog/founder-story-sales-handoff.html) is that job.

A way to review deals that does not require you to re-sell them.

## Who do you hire into that?

Not a chief, if the company is still one seller and a founder. A [fractional sales leader](/fractional-sales-leader/) is the closer description: someone who builds the process the first reps can run. A [fractional CRO vs a VP of sales](/fractional-cro-vs-vp-of-sales/) is a later argument, when a team already exists.

I do not recruit that person. If you want the motion built, that is [the 90-day engagement](/gtm-consulting.html), and it comes after the autopsy.
