Which founder should own sales is a choice between the people who already answer the phone. Two partners, or an owner and a working partner, can each be good at a piece of the deal and still leave the buyer with two answers.

## Which partner should own the number?

The one who can turn a bad job down, and who will write how you sell so the other partner, and later a seller, can run a deal. Being the stronger estimator does not hand you the number. Being the one customers like to call does not either, if every yes still lives only in your head.

[The founder-led sales trap](/problems/founder-led-sales-trap/) is what that looks like when nobody has been asked to own it.

## What sits in the revenue lane, and what stays with the other partner?

The sales owner decides which accounts you will chase, the price you will not go under, the story on the first call, and how open work gets reviewed. The partner who runs the shop, the crews, or the projects decides whether a date is real.

Settle the date before a proposal goes out. One conversation, held on purpose, before the document leaves.

Picture a commercial roofing company with two partners. One learned the business pricing jobs. One runs the crews. A general contractor calls, and both partners call back, each with a timeline. Choose the partner who owns the bid. The partner who owns the crews says whether the start is real before the number is sent. Picture a software company with two founders. One built the product. One talks to customers. A prospect asks what will be ready, and both answer. Choose the founder who owns the commercial conversation. The founder who owns the product says what is real before that answer is sent.

## How do you keep both of you off the same account?

One voice on each account. The other partner can join a meeting. One person sends the proposal and owns the next step. Two proposals teach the buyer to wait.

[How to get out of founder-led sales](/blog/how-to-get-out-of-founder-led-sales.html) is the writing that voice has to be able to hand to a seller later. [Handing the founder story to the team](/blog/founder-story-sales-handoff.html) is how that story gets told by someone who did not live it. Both of those assume you already know which founder the story belongs to.

## When should the revenue lane move off both founders?

When the motion is written down and the limit is the calendar. Then you are choosing a seller, or a [fractional sales leader](/fractional-sales-leader/) who builds the process that seller will run. A second founder staying in every deal is not that choice. I do not recruit the seller. I do not recruit the seller.
