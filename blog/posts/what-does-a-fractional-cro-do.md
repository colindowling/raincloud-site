A fractional CRO is a title for a part-time revenue operator. RAINCLOUD does not sell that seat. It builds a full-funnel demand system on the CRM you already have, and then it runs the system. It starts with a free 20 Minute Teardown, then the 100-Point GTM Autopsy. Nothing skips the autopsy.

The title is used loosely. Some buyers want a coach for the VP. Some want a firm to assign them a person. Some want the motion fixed and handed back. None of those is what I sell. If a fractional person has already come and gone, use the [questions to ask a fractional CRO](/blog/questions-to-ask-a-fractional-cro.html) before you take another meeting.

## What does the work look like?

The autopsy is the build specification. The build is the first month: access, domains and mailboxes, warmup, the TAM, CRM mapping, the content plan, and the first sequences. From go-live, RAINCLOUD runs mass email, bottom-of-funnel content, and signal outbound. The detail is on [the program page](/done-for-you-gtm/). The fees are on [the pricing page](/pricing/).

Four concurrent clients is the maximum. There is no bench. If you want a roster, that is a [network](/compare/fractional-cro-network-alternative/), and I am the alternative to that.

## What does a fractional CRO not do?

I do not carry a quota. I do not manage reps day to day. I do not recruit your next VP. I do not close your deals. RAINCLOUD keeps running the demand system. It does not hand it back at the end of a project and call that the product.

If the company is still one founder and a first seller, you may not need this title at all. Read [the fractional sales leader page](/fractional-sales-leader/) and [when the first sales hire is not working](/blog/first-sales-hire-not-working.html).

## Where should you start?

Book the [20 Minute Teardown](/teardown/). If it is useful, the next step is the [100-Point GTM Autopsy](/gtm-autopsy/): $5,000, five business days from access, required before the build, credited in full against the $25,000 build. There is no cash refund. The paid audit is the trial.
