How to create urgency when a prospect has no compelling event: stop leading with the feature they already worked around. Price the workaround they hate, in hours, delay, and risk, using their numbers. Then let them compare that figure with yours.

Assume the team has already found a way to live with the problem. They made it this far without you. The status quo is often the next best alternative, and it does not have a logo.

## Why does the feature pitch die?

You lead with the headache your product was built to remove. Nobody moves. Not because the headache is fake. Because they already routed around it. The route is ugly. It is also familiar, and it does not ask for budget.

If you cannot say what they do today, you are pitching into a blank. They are not in a blank. They are in a workaround.

## How do you price a workaround?

Ask how it works. Who touches it. What breaks when that person is out.

Ask what it costs. Hours. Slipped handoffs. The deal that waits because the spreadsheet is the system. People risk, when one person is the process.

Then compare, out loud, with your price. Their arithmetic, not yours. "This might be nicer" is not a date. "This consumes a person's time every month" can become one.

Some deals already have a compelling event: a renewal, an audit, a new leader, a target the current setup cannot carry. Ask about the next six months and listen for dates. If there is no date and the workaround is genuinely cheap, qualify out. Forcing it fills the forecast with hope. [Forecast accuracy](/blog/forecast-accuracy.html) dies that way. [Complaints are not demand](/blog/complaints-arent-demand.html) is the same test from the pain side.
