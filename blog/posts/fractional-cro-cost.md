Fractional CRO cost, on this site, means the fees I publish. I will not invent a salary, a day rate, or a market average for the category. Anyone who gives you a single number for "a fractional CRO" is averaging jobs that are not the same job.

What I charge is short. The 20 Minute Teardown is free. The 100-Point GTM Autopsy is $5,000 and takes ten business days. The 90-day engagement is $37,500.

## What do those numbers buy?

The teardown covers roughly 40 of the 100 points I can read from public sources. It is not the paid diagnostic.

The autopsy scores all 100 points against your CRM and your team. You get a baseline, every finding ranked by what it costs in pipeline, and a one-page business case. The $5,000 is credited in full toward any engagement, and it has a no-questions-asked money-back guarantee. [The autopsy page](/gtm-autopsy/) is the scope. [Pricing](/pricing/) is the schedule.

The 90 days are $15,000, then $12,500, then $10,000. Advisory is $7,500 a month from month four. The GTM Scorecard is $4,000 a month, billed quarterly, from month seven or directly after an autopsy. The six-month minimum is three months of build and three of advisory.

## What is not included in the price?

A full-time VP. A recruited placement. An agency that keeps sending after I leave. I do not manage your reps. [Fractional CRO vs VP of sales](/fractional-cro-vs-vp-of-sales/) is that line. [What a fractional CRO does here](/blog/what-does-a-fractional-cro-do.html) is the job, not the invoice.
