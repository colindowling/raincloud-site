RAINCLOUD vs Crayon

Crayon monitors the market.
RAINCLOUD analyzes your deals.

Crayon is a capable market intelligence platform that tracks competitor activity across public sources. It's useful for staying informed. But knowing what competitors are doing publicly and understanding how they're winning your deals are two different problems. RAINCLOUD solves the second one.

Where RAINCLOUD goes further.

Deal-level intelligence

Crayon tells you a competitor launched a new feature. RAINCLOUD tells you that feature is now mentioned in 23% of competitive calls and correlates with a 40% loss rate when your reps don't address it proactively.

Revenue attribution

Crayon helps you stay informed. RAINCLOUD quantifies competitive impact in dollars: how much pipeline is leaking, to whom, and which losses were preventable.

Proven talk tracks

Crayon can suggest messaging. RAINCLOUD delivers talk tracks extracted from deals your team actually won this quarter, scored by win rate and validated across hundreds of conversations.

Fully managed delivery

Crayon requires a CI team to curate and distribute intelligence. RAINCLOUD handles the entire pipeline from analysis to delivery. The intelligence arrives finished.
