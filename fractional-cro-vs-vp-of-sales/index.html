When is a VP the wrong first move?

When nobody can say who you sell to, what a qualified deal is, or why you win. A new VP inherits that fog and is judged on a number the motion cannot produce. The diagnostic is the 100-Point GTM Autopsy. The free look before it is the 20 Minute Teardown.

Questions people ask before they hire.

Should I hire a VP of sales or a fractional CRO?

Hire a VP when the motion already converts and the team is out of capacity. Bring in outside help when the motion itself is the problem. Adding a VP to a broken motion gives you a salary and the same forecast.

Does a fractional CRO replace a VP?

Not here. I do not manage your reps, carry a quota, or stay as the sales leader. After day 90 your team runs it. If you still need a VP, you hire one into a system that exists.

What does each one cost?
