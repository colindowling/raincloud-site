Fractional CRO vs VP of sales is a choice between a part-time operator and a full-time employee. A VP sits on your payroll and runs the team. I do not take that job. I score the motion, then RAINCLOUD builds the pipeline on your CRM and runs it.

When is a VP the wrong first move?

When nobody can say who you sell to, what a qualified deal is, or why you win. A new VP inherits that fog and is judged on a number the motion cannot produce. The diagnostic is the 100-Point GTM Autopsy. The free look before it is the 20 Minute Teardown.

Questions people ask before they hire.

Should I hire a VP of sales or a fractional CRO?

Hire a VP when the motion already converts and the team is out of capacity. Bring in outside help when the motion itself is the problem. Adding a VP to a broken motion gives you a salary and the same forecast.

Does a fractional CRO replace a VP?

Not here. I do not manage your reps, carry a quota, or stay as the sales leader. RAINCLOUD keeps running the pipeline. It does not manage your reps. If you still need a VP, you hire one into a system that exists.

What does each one cost?

I do not publish a VP salary, because I do not hire them and I will not invent a number. My fees are the free 20 Minute Teardown, a $5,000 100-Point GTM Autopsy, a $25,000 Build, then $10,000 a month. See the pricing page.

What if we are earlier than a VP?

If the founder is still the system, read the founder-led sales trap and the first sales hire page. A title will not substitute for a written process.
