Fractional sales leader

Looking for a fractional sales leader? Here’s the alternative where you keep the job.

A fractional sales leader is a senior seller who builds the process your first reps can run, then leaves it with them. Fractional sales leadership and a fractional CSO are the same search at this size.

I don’t build it for you and I don’t manage your reps. You keep the job; I make sure you’re doing it well. Anything we build, we build together on the call.

What is a sales process for a 50 person company?

A 50 person company rarely needs another chief. It needs a process a new seller can follow without the founder in the room.

  • Who you sell to, written tightly enough that a rep can disqualify an account.
  • What a qualified opportunity is, and the next step every open deal must have.
  • The story the founder tells, written down so the next person can tell it. Why founder-led sales stalls.
  • A first hire who is not asked to invent the system and hit a number in the same quarter. When the first sales hire fails.

The same idea, in a post: when the first sales hire is not working and how to get out of founder-led sales.

Is this you?

This is for you if

  • You own the business, or you may as well — the number is yours either way.
  • Revenue somewhere between $1M and $25M, B2B.
  • Your marketing is you, or one person, or an agency you’re not sure about.
  • Your sales team is you, or you and a couple of others.
  • You’ve read enough and watched enough. What you want is someone who has actually done it, in the room, once a week.
  • You intend to run this yourself. You’re not looking to hand it over.

This isn’t for you if

  • You want it done for you. I don’t do that any more, and I’m not going to be talked into it.
  • You want a guarantee on pipeline. The work is yours; so is the outcome.
  • You have a sales team that needs managing rather than a motion that needs designing. That’s a different problem and a different person.

Questions people ask before they hire.

What is a fractional sales leader?

A fractional sales leader builds the sales process, the message, and the first operating rhythm, then hands them to the people who will run them. I don’t. You build it with me in the room once a week, so nothing has to be handed over.

Do you manage the reps?

No. I don’t manage your reps. If you have a sales team that needs managing rather than a motion that needs designing, that’s a different problem and a different person.

Do you recruit the leader?

No. I do not place fractional executives and I do not run a bench. If you need a hire, that is your search.
