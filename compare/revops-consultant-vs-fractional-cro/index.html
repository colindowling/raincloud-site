What is RevOps vs sales ops?

RevOps vs sales ops is a boundary. Sales ops stops at the sales team: stages, fields, the forecast meeting. Revenue operations pulls marketing and customer success into the same definitions of lead, opportunity, and revenue. A sales operations consultant and a revenue operations consultant are shopping for that work. The published list calls the section RevOps, Data and Tooling. When is a revenue operations consultant the wrong first hire?

When the definitions are messy because the motion is messy. A cleaner CRM will report a bad process more quickly. If nobody can say who you sell to, fix that before you hire someone to enforce fields. That is the fractional CRO path on this site: score, then rebuild, then your team runs it. The 90 days are where the scorecard gets built in the CRM you already have.

What do I actually do with your data?

Read-only CRM access, from the autopsy onward. The scorecard is built where the data lives. I do not become your sales operations consultant, and I do not stay to run the reports after the build unless you keep advisory. Two builds at a time. The terms are on the pricing page.
