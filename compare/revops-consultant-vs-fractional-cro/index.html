What is RevOps vs sales ops?

RevOps vs sales ops is a boundary. Sales ops stops at the sales team: stages, fields, the forecast meeting. Revenue operations pulls marketing and customer success into the same definitions of lead, opportunity, and revenue. In the 100-Point Scorecard that work sits in Data, CRM and operations: 12 of the hundred points. The full list is in the 100-point framework.

When is a revenue operations consultant the wrong first hire?

When the definitions are messy because the motion is messy. A cleaner CRM will report a bad process more quickly. If nobody can say who you sell to, fix that before you hire someone to enforce fields.

Score it. Work it. Re-score it. The Standing Hour is where the work gets done, in the CRM you already have.

Do I need a CRM?

Not to start. You’ll need something before long, and setting it up properly is often one of the first things we do together.
