Why do people look for an alternative?

They bought a brand and met a stranger. Or the person was junior relative to the fee. Or the engagement had no end, so the company never had to run the motion itself.

Nobody outsources the thing their business runs on and comes out the other side owning it.

Why only six clients?

Because I do this myself, around a full-time role, and seven would make all seven worse. When it’s full it’s full, and if I tell you there’s a wait, there’s a wait.

Is this you?

This is for you if

  • You own the business, or you may as well — the number is yours either way.
  • Revenue somewhere between $1M and $25M, B2B.
  • Your marketing is you, or one person, or an agency you’re not sure about.
  • Your sales team is you, or you and a couple of others.
  • You’ve read enough and watched enough. What you want is someone who has actually done it, in the room, once a week.
  • You intend to run this yourself. You’re not looking to hand it over.

This isn’t for you if

  • You want it done for you. I don’t do that any more, and I’m not going to be talked into it.
  • You want a guarantee on pipeline. The work is yours; so is the outcome.
  • You have a sales team that needs managing rather than a motion that needs designing. That’s a different problem and a different person.
  • You won’t do the homework. Two or three things a week, every week. If that sounds like a lot, it isn’t going to work.

Questions people ask before they hire.

What is a fractional CRO network?

A network keeps a roster of operators and matches you to one. You often meet the person after you have bought the firm. The work and the seniority vary by who is assigned.

What is the alternative?

One named person. Six clients at a time. A fractional CRO runs your revenue function. I don’t run anything. You keep the job; I make sure you’re doing it well.

Is a network cheaper?

I do not know what a given network charges, and I will not invent a comparison rate. The 20 Minute Teardown is free. The 100-Point Scorecard is $5,000 standalone, or it takes month one at no extra cost. The Standing Hour is $4,500 a month.

What if I want a placement?

Then I am the wrong call. I do not place executives. If the motion is the problem, the Scorecard tells you that before you hire anyone.

Twenty minutes, free, and you keep the scorecard whether we work together or not. I’ll score forty of the hundred points from the outside, walk you through what I found, and leave you with two or three things to go and do.

Do them. If they help, we should talk about the other sixty.
