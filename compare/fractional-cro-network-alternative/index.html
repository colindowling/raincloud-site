Questions people ask before they hire.

What is a fractional CRO network?

A network keeps a roster of operators and matches you to one. You often meet the person after you have bought the firm. The work and the seniority vary by who is assigned.

What is the alternative?

One named person. I score the motion and, if we continue, we work it together an hour a week. There is no delivery team and no offshore bench. Six clients at a time.

Is a network cheaper?

I do not know what a given network charges, and I will not invent a comparison rate. My fees are published: a free teardown, the $5,000 Scorecard, and the Standing Hour at $4,500 a month.

What if I want a placement, not a rebuild?

Then I am the wrong call. I do not place executives. If the motion is the problem, the Scorecard tells you that before you hire anyone.
