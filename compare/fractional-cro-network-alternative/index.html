A network keeps a roster of operators and matches you to one. You often meet the person after you have bought the firm. The work and the seniority vary by who is assigned.

One named person who does the work. I score the motion and, if we continue, I rebuild it. There is no delivery team and no offshore bench. I run two builds at a time.
