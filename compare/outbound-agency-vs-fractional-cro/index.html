The symptoms people hire an agency for

  • Outbound that burns out. You send, it works for a month, then it stops. No trigger telling you who’s worth contacting this week, and a target list wide enough that nothing you write lands as specific.
  • Channels that run in parallel instead of together. Email, content, LinkedIn, referrals — four separate efforts with four separate lists, none of them compounding into the others.

These aren’t five problems. They’re five symptoms of a motion that was assembled a piece at a time and never designed. Which is also why fixing them in the wrong order didn’t work.

The longer version is outbound that is not landing and why outbound is not working.

When is an agency the wrong next invoice?

When you cannot say who the list is for, what a reply is supposed to do next, or which stage loses the deal. An agency will still send. The market will still remember the noise. Volume pointed the wrong way is not a bargain.

I will not name agencies, and I will not pretend a category is a villain. Some teams need more activity.

What I don’t do

  • I don’t run your outbound, write your content, or manage your campaigns.
  • I don’t build or maintain anything between sessions.
  • I don’t manage your reps.
  • I don’t book meetings or carry a number on your behalf.

And I don’t guarantee a revenue outcome, because the work is yours and so is the result. If you want someone to be accountable for your pipeline instead of you, I’m the wrong call — and I’d rather say that here than three months in.

What you own

Everything. Every list, every agent, every sequence, every document, every framework. All of it gets built in the session, on your screen, in your accounts.

If we stop working together, nothing breaks and nothing has to be handed over, because there was never anything on my side to hand.

Questions people ask before they hire.

Should I hire an outbound agency or a fractional CRO?

Hire an agency when the targeting, the offer, and the follow-up already work and you need more of that activity. A fractional CRO runs your revenue function. If you intend to run it yourself, the alternative is the Standing Hour: you keep the job; I make sure you’re doing it well.

What is a demand generation agency alternative?

Not another agency. The alternative is to find which stage is leaking before you buy more top-of-funnel. That is the 20 Minute Teardown, then the 100-Point Scorecard if the teardown is useful.

Will you run our outbound?

No. I don’t run your outbound, write your content, or manage your campaigns. Anything we build, we build together on the call.

What if the agency is not the problem?

Then the Scorecard will say so. The Scorecard stands on its own and plenty of people take it and go do the work themselves.
