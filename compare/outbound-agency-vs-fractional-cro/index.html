Comparison

Outbound agency vs fractional CRO, and the demand generation agency alternative.

Outbound agency vs fractional CRO is activity versus ownership. An agency sends the sequences and counts the meetings. A fractional CRO is accountable for the motion those meetings have to fit. If you want a demand generation agency alternative, I do not replace the agency with another one.

When is an agency the wrong next invoice?

When you cannot say who the list is for, what a reply is supposed to do next, or which stage loses the deal. An agency will still send. The market will still remember the noise. Volume pointed the wrong way is not a bargain.

What I will not do

I will not name agencies, and I will not pretend a category is a villain. Some teams need more activity. I need the motion to be able to use it. If it cannot, the agency is not the first invoice.

Questions people ask before they hire.

Should I hire an outbound agency or a fractional CRO?

Hire an agency when the targeting, the offer, and the follow-up already work and you need more of that activity. Bring in diagnosis when reply rates fell and you have been rewriting sequences. More activity on a broken motion just spends the list faster.

What is a demand generation agency alternative?

Not another agency. The alternative is to find which stage is leaking before you buy more top-of-funnel. That is the 20 Minute Teardown, then the 100-Point GTM Autopsy if the teardown is useful.

Will you run our outbound?

During the 90 days I build the outbound: list sources, sequences, and deliverability, in your tools. I do not stay on to send it, manage the reps, or replace an agency as the operator. Your team runs it after day 90.

What if the agency is not the problem?

Then the autopsy will say so. You keep the score either way. Plenty of companies take the diagnosis and keep their agency, with a clearer target.
