Where pipeline actually leaks

More pipeline won't fix a leak. It just leaks faster.

A bad quarter usually gets the same answer: more leads, more sequences, more reps. That's pouring water into a bucket without looking for the hole.

  1. 01
    Coverage tells you what went in. It says nothing about where it's coming out. The board sees 4x and relaxes. The forecast says otherwise.
  2. 02
    Every stage leaks a little. One stage leaks a lot. And it's rarely the one leadership blames. Marketing blames sales, sales blames leads, and the data quietly points somewhere else.
  3. 03
    The margin for a leaky funnel is gone. Ebsta and Pavilion's 2025 benchmark put the new-logo win rate at 19%, down from 29% a year earlier. At 19%, you need more than 5x coverage just to hit plan. (Ebsta × Pavilion, 2025 GTM Benchmarks)
  4. 04
    Which is why one fix beats ten initiatives. Find the stage. Fix the mechanism. Measure the change. Then go find the next one.
