What does a fractional CRO actually do?

The title gets used for three different jobs. Only one of them is this.

  • Some people want a part-time executive who sits in the forecast and coaches the VP. That is a seat.
  • Some want a firm that sends a bench. That is a network. How a network differs.
  • I score the motion, rebuild it in 90 days, and hand it to your team. The fee steps down because the work changes hands. How the 90 days work.

If you are comparing the title with a full-time VP, read fractional CRO vs VP of sales. If the company is closer to fifty people and one seller, start with a fractional sales leader instead.

Who a fractional CRO is for

B2B companies between $50 million and $200 million, with a sales team in a CRM, missing a number they have already committed to. One executive owns the work for six months. Below the range is sometimes a fit. Above it there is usually an internal team.
