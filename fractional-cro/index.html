Fractional CRO

A fractional CRO, without the open-ended seat.

A fractional CRO is a senior revenue operator who works part of the time, not a full-time chief revenue officer on your payroll. I do not sell that seat. You start with the free 20 Minute Teardown, then the 100-Point Scorecard, then a standing hour every week while you run it.

What does a fractional CRO actually do?

The title gets used for three different jobs. Only one of them is this.

  • Some people want a part-time executive who sits in the forecast and coaches the VP. That is a seat.
  • Some want a firm that sends a bench. That is a network. How a network differs.
  • I score your go-to-market against 100 dimensions, we work the gaps in the order that matters, and everything we build is yours. How the Standing Hour works.

If you are comparing the title with a full-time VP, read fractional CRO vs VP of sales. If the company is closer to fifty people and one seller, start with a fractional sales leader instead. If the search is a RevOps seat, read RevOps consultant vs a fractional CRO.

Who a fractional CRO is for

Owner-led B2B companies, revenue somewhere between $1M and $25M. Your sales team is you, or you and a couple of others. You intend to run this yourself. You’re not looking to hand it over.
