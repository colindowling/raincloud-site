How the two weeks break down.

From the moment you grant access to the moment your leadership team sees the findings. No disruption. No change management. No new tools to learn.

Day 1

Kickoff & access

30-minute call. We align on your market, your motion, and the questions you need answered. You grant read-only access to your sequencer, CRM, and analytics. We start the same day.

Your lift: 30 minutes
Days 2–8

The teardown

We pull apart the whole motion. Is the ICP tight enough to build a system around? Do the offers and angles hold up? Is outbound structurally sound or is it burning the list? Does the content map to buyer intent or is it a publishing calendar? Where is the positioning soft?

Your lift: Zero
Days 9–12

Diagnosis & sequencing

Findings become a ranked plan. Not a list of everything wrong — an ordered plan of what to fix first, what to fix second, and what to deliberately ignore for now. Sequencing is the part most operators get wrong.

Your lift: Zero
Day 13–14

Findings session

60-minute session with your leadership team. We walk the diagnosis, the ranked plan, and the tradeoffs behind the order. You leave knowing exactly what's broken and what fixing it actually takes.

Your lift: 60 minutes

What you walk away with.

Not a report that sits in a drawer. A diagnosis you can act on immediately — and the foundation of the 26-week build if you choose to continue.

GTM Diagnosis

A plain-language assessment of your motion end to end. What's working, what's broken, what's missing entirely, and which of those is actually costing you pipeline right now. Written for a leadership audience, not a data dump.

ICP Assessment

Whether your stated ICP matches the accounts you actually win. Segment-level conversion, the profile of your best closed-won deals, and how tight the definition needs to get before a system can be built on it.

Offer & Angle Audit

Your current offers pressure-tested against the market. Which angles have legs, which are interchangeable with every competitor, and where the 5x5 matrix is thin. Targeting is only as good as what you're targeting them with.

Channel Teardown

Outbound, content, and the process holding them together, assessed individually and as a system. Where each prong is leaking, where deals are falling through the gaps, and whether any of it is amplifying the rest.

Content Gap Analysis

What you're publishing versus what your buyers are searching. Keyword targets you're missing, where the bottom-of-funnel anchor should sit, and which pages would actually make outbound more credible.

Ranked Action Plan

Not "you should improve your GTM." Specific, ordered actions with the reasoning behind the sequence. What to fix in the first 30 days, what waits until the foundation holds, and what to stop doing entirely.

Common questions.

What if our outbound isn't running yet?

That's fine, and it's common. If there's no motion to audit, the work shifts toward foundation — ICP definition, offer construction, and channel selection. We'll tell you on the kickoff call which version of the audit you're getting.

Is our data secure?

We access your systems read-only and never export raw customer data. The deliverable is analysis and recommendations, not a copy of your CRM. Happy to sign an NDA and work within your security requirements.

How is this different from a free GTM assessment?

A free assessment is a sales call with a slide at the end. This is two weeks of work inside your actual systems, and the deliverable is a sequenced plan you can execute with or without us. The money-back guarantee is the difference in confidence.

Do we need to pause anything or change our process?

No. The audit runs in the background. We need read-only access and a 30-minute kickoff. Your team keeps selling. Nothing changes until you decide to change it based on what we find.

Who actually does the work?

Colin does. There is no junior analyst and no offshore team. That's also why availability is limited to a small number of companies at a time.

What if the audit doesn't find anything useful?

Then you don't pay. If you aren't floored by what we find, we refund the full $7,500. We've never had a motion come through clean, but the guarantee is real and it's yours to call.

What if $60,000 isn’t in the budget right now?

Then say so on the call. The audit itself is a standalone $7,500 engagement that helps define where you are in your GTM journey and what to prioritize next to accelerate growth. There is no obligation to continue. And there is a lighter ongoing option underneath the build: monthly guidance and coaching starting at $3,000, where I am on the call with you rather than in the build. It is a different product with a different promise, and for some companies at this stage it is the honest recommendation.
