Common questions.

Why is the audit required?

Because I won’t sell a six-month engagement off a sales call and you shouldn’t buy one. Ten days and $5,000 is how we both find out whether this is worth doing.

Why isn’t the audit credited toward the build?

Because then it isn’t an audit, it’s a deposit. The audit is a real piece of work with its own deliverable, and plenty of companies take the diagnosis and run the fix themselves.

Can we skip the audit if we already know what’s wrong?

No. You may well be right about what’s wrong — most people are right about one of the five and miss the other four.

What if our outbound isn’t running yet?

That’s fine, and it’s common. It gets scored like everything else, and an outbound motion that doesn’t exist is a finding in itself. I’ll tell you on the kickoff call where the weight of the audit will sit.

Is our data secure?

I access your systems read-only and never export raw customer data. The deliverable is analysis and recommendations, not a copy of your CRM. I’m happy to sign an NDA and work within your security requirements.

How is this different from a free GTM assessment?

A free assessment is a sales call with a slide at the end. This is ten business days of work inside your actual systems, scored against 100 published points, and the deliverable is a sequenced plan you can execute with or without me.

Who actually does the work?

I do. There is no junior analyst and no offshore team. That’s also why I run two builds at a time.
