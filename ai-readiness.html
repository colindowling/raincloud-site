FAQ

Questions worth asking before you engage.

If something is not answered here, ask it in the intake call. I would rather you go in clear than uncertain.

How is this different from hiring a big consulting firm to do our AI strategy?

Large firms send a senior partner to sell the work, then hand it to a team of analysts you have never met. The thinking you are paying for gets diluted before it reaches you. This engagement is different by design: you work directly with me from intake to delivery, and the strategy is built around your business, not adapted from a template. You also get a fixed fee and a fixed timeline. No billable-hour surprises, no scope drift, and no 200-page deck that sits unread until the next planning cycle.

We already have some AI initiatives underway. Is this still relevant?

Often more relevant. Most organizations running AI initiatives are doing so without a clear outcomes framework, without defined ownership, and without a coherent view of how the pieces fit together. The readiness assessment is built to diagnose exactly that: where you have real momentum versus where you have the appearance of progress. If your pilots are not converting to enterprise-wide impact, that is a leadership and governance problem, not a technology problem. That is what I fix.

What do we actually walk away with at the end of 30 days?

Five concrete deliverables, all owned by you permanently: a written AI readiness assessment documenting your current state across five dimensions; an outcomes-first AI strategy aligned to your business priorities; a tool and vendor evaluation framework you can use independently; a 90-day execution blueprint with sequenced milestones, owners, and decision gates; and a board-ready AI narrative you can present with confidence. Everything is written, structured, and handed over, not locked in my head or dependent on ongoing access to me.

How much of our team's time does this require?

Less than you would expect, on purpose. For the Solo Executive engagement, plan on roughly 4 to 5 hours of your time across the full 30 days: the initial intake session, one or two working sessions during the strategy build, and the final walkthrough. For the Leadership Trio and Full C-Suite tiers, individual executive time stays similar. The additional time is in the cross-functional alignment sessions, which are structured and facilitated so they do not sprawl.

What happens after the engagement ends? Is there ongoing support?

Every engagement includes a period of async Q&A access after delivery: 30, 45, or 60 days depending on your tier. Beyond that, I do not push retainers as a default. Some clients continue with me into implementation, often through GTM consulting or AI agents; others take the strategy and execute on their own. What matters is that you leave with everything you need to move without me.

We already have a Chief AI Officer or a Head of Innovation. Do we still need this?

Possibly the most common situation I see, and one of the most useful applications of this engagement. A Chief AI Officer is responsible for execution. What this engagement addresses is executive alignment above that layer: whether the C-suite and board are asking the right questions, whether strategy is being set at the leadership level rather than delegated to a single internal champion, and whether the organization has a coherent framework the CAIO is executing against, or just a mandate with no guardrails. It also gives your innovation leader something to point to when they need air cover.
