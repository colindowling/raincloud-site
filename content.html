    Track 2: ghostwriting

    Ghostwriting that drives growth, not traffic.

    AI slop is polluting the internet, and that is a big opportunity for you. More content is being created than ever before, and most of it is garbage. Great content with an original point of view cuts through better than ever, earning the trust of potential buyers and the attention of crawlers from Google, Microsoft, OpenAI, and Anthropic.

    “We could just write this with Claude.”

    The objection I hear most. Every business and agency is thinking that ChatGPT or Claude is good enough to produce their content. The mistake is not recognizing that good enough no longer cuts it with customers, search engines, and answer engines. LLMs are fantastic for research, a brief, an outline, and even a first draft. But if you want content that represents your brand voice, resonates with readers, ranks, and leads to growth, you need a human subject-matter expert at the end of the production line to edit, polish, and enhance it.

    / 01

    The baseline

    When every competitor can produce competent AI content, competent stops being an edge. It is the entry fee. The bar just moved up, not down.

    / 02

    The judgment call

    A model has no opinion on which of your offers deserves the next six weeks of content, or which claim is technically true but wrong for this buyer. That call gets made by someone who has watched what actually closes.

    / 03

    The hours

    If your most expensive person is the one prompting the model, that is a six-figure employee doing the job of an intern. The real question was never whether AI can write it. It is who is holding the pen.

    What I do differently

    Everyone is using AI now. Here is where I draw the line.

    Pretending otherwise would be its own kind of slop. The honest question was never whether AI touches the work. It is what it is allowed to decide.

    Signal

    First I look at competitors, market dynamics, review sites, and sales conversations (if allowed) to find hidden opportunities to reach buyers in their own language.

    Structure

    Every page is built for three readers: the human, a search crawler, and an answer engine. Headings, schema, and passages that can be lifted cleanly, so the piece can be found and cited, not just published.

    Pathing

    Internal links are placed with purpose. Each one moves the reader further down your funnel, not into a “related posts” dead end.

    Coverage

    I keep a live map of what competitors are saying and, as important, what they are not saying. Those gaps inform a strategy optimized in real time.

    None of that is where the writing actually happens. That part still needs someone in the room who knows what is true about your business, your buyer, and your last ten deals, plus the discipline to say only that.
